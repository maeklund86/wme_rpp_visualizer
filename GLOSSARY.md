# WME RPP Visualizer

This context covers Residential Point Places, their entry points, and the road connections shown in Waze Map Editor.

## Language

**Residential Point Place (RPP)**:
A Waze map place representing a residential address.

**Entry point**:
A navigation point associated with an RPP, representing an entry or exit location.

**Road segment**:
A mapped road geometry used to show a connection to an RPP or its entry point.

**Connection-eligible road segment**:
A road segment that may be selected for an RPP road connection. Walking trails, pedestrian boardwalks, walkways, and stairways are not eligible.

**Annotation**:
A map marking that visualizes an RPP, its entry point, or its connection to a road.
